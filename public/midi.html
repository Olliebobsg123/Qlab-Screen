Keyboard on an iPhone or iPad

Checking…

This server runs its own network MIDI session called QLab Connect. Anything that plays into it goes straight to QLab's MIDI triggers. You don't need to set anything up on the Mac.

  1. Plug the keyboard into the iPhone. On older iPhones use Apple's Lightning to USB 3 Camera Adapter with its charging port powered.
  2. Install a free network MIDI app from the App Store, such as midimittr.
  3. Open the app, allow local network access, and connect to QLab Connect (choose RTP, not Network MIDI 2.0).
  4. Play a key. The phone appears below and the notes go to QLab.
