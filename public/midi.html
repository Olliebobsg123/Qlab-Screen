How to connect a keyboard through an iPhone
  1. Plug the keyboard into the iPhone (older iPhones: Apple's Lightning to USB 3 Camera Adapter, with its charging port powered).
  2. Open a network MIDI app such as midimittr and turn its network session on (RTP).
  3. The phone appears in the list above. Press Connect. It reconnects by itself after that.
