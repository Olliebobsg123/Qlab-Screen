Departments

Each department logs in at /login.html and controls only its own cues

Give each department its own cue list in QLab (for example “Lighting”) and pick it here. You can also match cues by colour or by how their name or number starts (e.g. LX). If you choose a cue list and a colour/prefix, only cues in that list that also match count. Departments need QLab control switched on (below).
